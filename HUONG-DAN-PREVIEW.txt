HUONG DAN PREVIEW WEBSITE TREN DIEN THOAI (GitHub Actions tu dong deploy)

BUOC 1: Dua toan bo thu muc nay len GitHub (giu ca thu muc .github)

BUOC 2: Bat Pages bang GitHub Actions (chi lam 1 lan)
  - Repo > Settings > Pages
  - Muc "Build and deployment" > Source: chon "GitHub Actions"
    (KHONG chon "Deploy from a branch" nua)

BUOC 3: Chay deploy
  - Repo > tab Actions > "Deploy Preview (GitHub Pages)"
  - Tu chay sau moi lan push len nhanh main, hoac bam Run workflow de chay tay
  - Khi co dau tich xanh (khoang 1 phut), bam vao lan chay do:
    link website hien o muc "deploy" > github-pages
  - Link co dang: https://TEN_GITHUB.github.io/the-gioi-be-thong-minh/

BUOC 4: Mo link tren dien thoai
  - Chrome: menu ⋮ > Them vao man hinh chinh
  - Safari: nut Chia se > Them vao Man hinh chinh

Neu bao loi "Get Pages site failed" hoac "not enabled":
  vao Settings > Pages va lam lai Buoc 2, roi bam "Re-run jobs".
